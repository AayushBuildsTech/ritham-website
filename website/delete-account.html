Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated · September 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Ritham (com.ritham.app) account and all the data associated with it. You can do this yourself inside the app, or request it from us directly — no app required.

1. Delete from inside the app (instant)

1. Open Ritham and sign in. 2. Go to Settings → Delete Account. 3. Confirm. Your account and all associated data are permanently erased immediately. This cannot be undone.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you can no longer access the app, email us from the address you used to sign in (your Google account email) at rithamastro@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Ritham account”. We verify ownership of the email and then permanently delete your account and data. We acknowledge requests within 24 hours and complete deletion within 15 days, in line with applicable Indian law (the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

3. What gets deleted

Deletion permanently erases everything tied to your account, including: • Your profile — name, email and birth details (date, time and place of birth). • Your chat conversations and AI voice-call history. • Your reports and any files you uploaded (such as palm photos or Vaastu floor plans). • Your numerology, dream and other saved readings. • Your purchase and entitlement records.

4. What may be retained

We do not retain your personal content after deletion. A limited set of records may be kept only where the law requires it — for example, transaction/tax records held by our payment processor (Razorpay) for the statutory period. These are retained for legal compliance only and are not used to identify or contact you. Payment card details are never stored by us at any time.

Questions about deletion? Contact rithamastro@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy for full details on how we handle your data.
