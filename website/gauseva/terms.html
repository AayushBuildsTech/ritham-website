Last updated: 21 August 2026

This is a paid seva service, not a donation. When you book a Gau Seva you are purchasing a service that Ritham arranges on your behalf — the feeding and care of cows (gau mata) performed in your name, together with a video delivered to you as proof. Your payment is a service fee, not a charitable donation. A portion of it funds the fodder and care offered to the cows; the remainder covers Ritham's service, coordination and operating costs. Ritham is not a charitable trust or NGO, and this payment does not qualify for any tax exemption, 80G benefit, or donation receipt.

1. What you are booking

Ritham (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a service provider that coordinates gau seva — the feeding and care of cows — which is physically carried out at a goshala (cow shelter), including through partner goshalas and associates. You are not contracting directly with the goshala; you are booking a service from Ritham, which arranges for the seva to be performed in your name. Each seva tier (₹11, ₹51, ₹101, ₹251, ₹501) represents the scope of seva your booking supports, shown as a “Seva” count on the page.

2. How your payment is used

The amount you pay is a single, all-inclusive service fee for arranging and documenting the seva. It is not held or forwarded by us as a donation. Broadly, a portion of the fee is applied to the cost of the fodder and care actually offered to the cows on your behalf, and the remainder covers Ritham's services — including coordination with the goshala, arrangement of the seva, filming and delivery of your proof video, payment-processing charges, support and general operating costs. The exact split may vary between bookings, goshalas and seva tiers, and we do not represent that any fixed proportion is passed on. By booking, you acknowledge and accept this arrangement.

3. Proof video & delivery

After your payment is confirmed, the seva is arranged and a video of the seva is delivered to you on WhatsApp within 7 days. The video is shared to the WhatsApp number linked to your payment, so please ensure the contact number you provide at checkout is correct and reachable on WhatsApp. The video is provided as reasonable proof that seva was performed; because seva for multiple devotees may be carried out together at the goshala, the footage may show a shared feeding session rather than an individually isolated clip for each booking.

4. Payment & pricing

All bookings are prepaid through a secure payment page (UPI / cards, powered by Razorpay). We do not collect or store your card details, and there is no cash on delivery. Prices are in Indian Rupees (₹) and are inclusive of the charges shown at checkout. We may revise seva tiers or pricing at any time; the price applicable to your booking is the one displayed when you pay.

5. Refunds & cancellations

  • Because seva is arranged shortly after payment, a booking generally cannot be cancelled or refunded once the seva has been performed and the video prepared, as the service has then been rendered.
  • If the seva cannot be performed or the video cannot be delivered within a reasonable time for a reason on our side, you may request a full refund of that booking.
  • Duplicate or accidental payments, and payments that failed but were still debited, are refunded in full on verification.
  • To raise a refund or delivery query, contact us on WhatsApp (or the email below) with your payment reference. Approved refunds are made to the original payment method, typically within 5–7 working days.
