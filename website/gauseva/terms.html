Gau Seva — Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This is a paid seva service, not a donation. When you book a Gau Seva, you are paying for a service — the feeding and care of cows (gau mata) performed in your name, and a video delivered to you as proof. It is not a charitable donation, and it does not qualify for any tax exemption or 80G benefit.

1. What you are booking

Ritham (“we”, “us”) arranges gau seva — the feeding and care of cows — on your behalf. Each seva tier (₹11, ₹51, ₹101, ₹251, ₹501) represents the scope of seva your payment supports, shown as a “Seva” count on the page. You are booking a service to be performed in your name.

2. Proof video & delivery

After your payment is confirmed, the seva is performed and a video of the seva is delivered to you on WhatsApp within 7 days. The video is shared to the WhatsApp number linked to your payment. Please ensure the contact number you provide at payment is correct and reachable on WhatsApp.

3. Payment

All bookings are prepaid via a secure hosted payment page (UPI / cards, powered by Razorpay). We do not collect or store your card details. There is no cash on delivery. Prices are in Indian Rupees (₹) and inclusive of applicable charges shown at checkout.

4. Refunds & cancellations

  • Once a seva has been performed and the video prepared, the booking is not refundable, as the service has been rendered.
  • If, for any reason on our side, the seva cannot be performed and the video cannot be delivered within a reasonable time, you may request a full refund of that booking.
  • To raise a refund or delivery query, contact us on WhatsApp (or the email below) with your payment reference. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method.

This section is read together with Ritham's general Refund & Cancellation policy.

5. Nature of the offering

Gau seva is a devotional service undertaken in good faith. References to blessings, punya and merit are expressions of faith and tradition; we make no guarantee of any specific spiritual, material or personal outcome. Photographs and any sample media on the site are illustrative.

6. Your responsibilities

  • Provide an accurate, WhatsApp-enabled contact number at payment so your video can be delivered.
  • Book only with your own payment method and with lawful funds.

7. Contact

For any question about a booking, delivery or refund, message us on WhatsApp via the button on the Gau Seva page, or email rithamastro@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 2–3 working days.
