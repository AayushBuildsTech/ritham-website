Why Gau Seva

The cow is a mother to all

In the Sanatan tradition, the cow — Gau Mata — is revered as a giver of nourishment and life. To feed and protect a cow is among the gentlest and most cherished acts of seva. It asks nothing of you but a little kindness, and it returns blessings that stay with your family.

गावो विश्वस्य मातरः “The cows are the mothers of the universe.” To serve them is to serve every home they nourish.
