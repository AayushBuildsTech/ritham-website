Why Gau Seva

The cow is a mother to all

In the Sanatan tradition, the cow — Gau Mata — is revered as a giver of nourishment and life. To feed and protect a cow is among the gentlest and most cherished acts of seva — it asks only a little kindness, and returns blessings that stay with your family.

गावो विश्वस्य मातरः “The cows are the mothers of the universe.” To serve them is to serve every home they nourish.

Frequently asked

Will I really get a video? +
Yes. Every seva includes a genuine video of the seva performed in your name, sent on WhatsApp within 7 days of booking.
How is the seva done? +
Your payment funds the feeding and care of cows (gau mata) — fodder, nourishment and upkeep. A larger seva supports more cows.
When is the seva performed? +
The seva is performed after you book, and your video reaches you within 7 days. Bookings for this round are closing soon.
Where do the cows live and who cares for them? +
The cows are cared for at a goshala (cow shelter) by people devoted to their wellbeing. Your video shows the very seva your booking supported. For anything specific, message us on WhatsApp.
Is this a donation? +
No. Gau Seva is a paid seva service: you book a seva, it is performed on your behalf, and you receive a video as proof. It is not a charitable donation and carries no tax-exemption (80G) benefit. See our Gau Seva Terms.
Is my payment secure? +
Yes — payment is prepaid via a secure hosted UPI / card page. We never see or store your card details. No cash on delivery.
